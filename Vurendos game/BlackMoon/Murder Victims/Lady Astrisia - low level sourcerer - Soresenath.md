#BlackMoon
![[Lady Astrisia.png]]
She's a low level sorceress who is, in private, a massive advocate for abolishing slavery. She has "slaves" but she treats them VERY well behind closed doors. 

For Example, they get proper food and clothing, and she also allots them an allowance and gets things they want on their behalf. 

She even has gotten a bit closer to one of her slaves in particular, though again this is mostly in private.

Lady Astrisia,  survived due to Sylvia ending her profession. However the BlackMoon sent another assassin to take her out. Ashwin fought off the assassin but was unable to apprehend them.

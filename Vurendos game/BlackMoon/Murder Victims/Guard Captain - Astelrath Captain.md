#BlackMoon
![[Guard Captainn - Andreas.png]]
The Guard Captain (Andreas) died on the border of Astelrath in Fesara. He was smuggling slaves from other countries into Astelrath so that they could get their legal citizenship to get them out of slavery.

The killer left a white rose instead of a black rose, it seems to be the same killer due to how Andreas died and for the reasons. However the killer did not seem to want to kill the Captain. When speak with dead was casted he said the killer did not want to do this even leaving the slave that the Captain was protecting alive.

The Guard Captain supported the abolishment of slavery. He was also found without any wounds only a bloody noise. - This indicated the same killer as well as the use of psychic magic to effect the targets brain. The only difference in this murder was the sudden change in calling card from a black rose to a white rose.

#BlackMoon
![[Fenuir - Official of New Castellius.png]]
Fenuir lived in New Castellius within Astelraths borders. 

Fenuir was an important Official, who was against slavery actively apposing it, he was a trusted official within Astelrath being the steward of this city his death being quite major, the killer used the black rose of Famis as a calling card stealing some from the church within New Castellius using one of the grounds keepers robes to sneak in and cut a flower. 

We spoke to several plant life through Bryyair to get a rough description of the killer but not enough to go on, similar to one of the party members physic.

He was also found without any wounds only a bloody noise. - This indicated the same killer as well as the use of psychic magic to effect the targets brain.

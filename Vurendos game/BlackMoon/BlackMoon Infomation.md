#BlackMoon

The Black Moon is an assassins organisation for hire. They kill anyone for the right price.

The Black Moon hide behind another organisation. This organisation is called The Hidden Promise, they are a mercenary's guild used as a front to avoid further investigation on anyone who may wish to uncover their secrets.

Very little members within the Black Moon know who the leader is.

The Black Moon, remove memories of loved ones to stop people from having attachments and to make them loyal to the Black Moon.

#Kalyth  #Powerup

**First power up** 
You feel your devotion to Famis swell within you, as you know who shall guide you proper. Your Zweihander becomes a +1 weapon, you gain the extra attack feature. You then gain one of the following:

**Blossom and Bloom** Whenever a creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points within 30ft, you may summon an Avatar of Roses. The Avatar of Roses statblock is listed along with this, and lasts until it perishes or you attempt to re-summon it. The Avatar of Roses rolls its own initiative, however you may freely dictate its turn. You cannot attempt to re-summon the Avatar of Roses to restore the amount of Blossoms it can use, and if your Avatar of Roses dies, the next one starts with half of its maximum hit points until a long rest.

**Second power up**
Medicus has chosen you to gain his favor himself, and he has put in a good word with his neice. Your Zweihander becomes a +2 weapon, and you gain a Supreme Potion of Healing. Every time you use it, it refills itself at the end of your next long rest. You then gain one of the following:

**Floral Squire** The Avatar of Roses gains class levels equal to half of your level (rounded up). It can take any class it wishes, and can multi-class so long as it meets the requirements to do so.

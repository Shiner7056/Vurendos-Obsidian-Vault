## Notes
Physical Description:
6’2ft Long slicked back hair, athletic build, radiant teal coloured eyes. Tattoos cover his arms and the portions of his legs that show beneath his heavy armour. Many are of ravens, representing his home town of Ravenford.

Pierce was condemned for a crime and found guilty against it, he was deemed redeemable and was allowed to keep his life.

Pierce grew up within the Republic of Poskaris before it fell into rebellions. Living in a town called Ravenford that was deep within the forests of the republic of Poskaris.

Pierce served in the Jethal rebellion against the republic of Posarkis. After the war Pierce served under a newly appointed lord named Kaleb, working as one of his personal guards. All records recounted Kaleb's rule as peaceful within Ravenford.

During Lord Kaleb's wedding rumours spread around Pierce arguing with Lord Kaleb about killing their future.

Public records describe the wedding to have been a disaster. A massacre. Records state that Pierce was not the cause of the deaths and that a witch had brought something into the venue that caused the deaths.

The main reason Pierce was ruled out as a suspect was due to the relationship he had with Lord Kaleb as well as the many commendations he had acquired throughout his service to Ravenford.

## Fey’re — Dream Feeding

- **Consent:** Someone can consent to Fey’re feeding, but dreams can reveal things the dreamer doesn't consciously want revealed. Most people cannot control their dreams.
- **How she feeds:**
    - Does **not need physical contact**.
    - Can sit beside or lean over someone while they sleep.
    - Reaches into the edges of their dream and observes it.
    - Dreams feel **warm and alive** to her.
    - She experiences what the dreamer experiences: tastes, smells, emotions, sensations, imagery, etc.
    - She consumes the dream, drawing it toward herself as a **silver mist** that she inhales.
- **What she consumes first:** Colours → emotions → sound → sensations → faces → surroundings.
- **Effect on Fey’re:** Feeding makes her feel **“wonderfully, horribly alive.”**
- **Effect on the dreamer:**
    - Usually wakes knowing they slept and dreamed but **cannot remember the dream**.
    - Weaker-minded individuals can be affected more severely.
    - Stronger minds generally wake with their dream completely emptied.
    - They may be left with an **empty, dreamless void**.

### Feeding on Fear

- Individuals with **lower mental fortitude** react differently.
- Fear, anxiety, adrenaline, hysteria and panic are **stronger and more potent** to Fey’re.
- Fear provides significantly more sustenance than positive emotions.
- The instinctive terror of **wanting to flee but having nowhere to go** is particularly potent.
- The more frightened someone becomes, **the more Fey’re can consume**.
- Fear almost **burns** when she feeds on it.
- Fey’re finds resisting the urge to consume fear **extremely difficult**, despite knowing it causes harm.
- She usually experiences **guilt afterward**.

### Consequences

- Victims may wake:
    - Screaming
    - Crying
    - Remembering that they had a nightmare but **not what frightened them**
- They may be left with only the lingering sensation that **they were afraid**.
- With weaker minds, Fey’re may consume everything except the fear.
- In those cases, **fear is sometimes the only thing left in the dream**.

### Important Character Note

Fey’re **doesn't describe this with pride**. She feels genuine guilt and discomfort about what feeding does to people, particularly when fear is involved. Her hunger and instinct to consume are extremely difficult to resist, creating a conflict between **what she needs to survive and the harm that survival can cause**.

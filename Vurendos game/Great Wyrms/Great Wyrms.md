#Great-Wyrms
## **Ignaris the Blazing** 
Father of the Red and attuned to the plane of Fire. Ignaris is hot-headed and has an ill-temper. He is quick to anger and quick to destroy everything in his path, and during the age of the Eladrin Ignaris set half of the world to such an unbearable summer that dozens of wildfire would spring up out of complete nowhere. Ignaris upon his defeat was petrified and sealed away in the hottest pit of the world.
![[Ignaris the Blazing.png]]

## Frozeron the Spiteful
Father of the Whites and attuned to the plane of Water (Ice) Frozeron is cold and calculating, using the terrain to his advantage he prowls frozen tundra's and strikes when his foes are un-prepared. His lair is decorated with the frozen corpses of his greatest enemies. During the age of the Eladrin Frozeron set half of the world into an eternal blizzard, freezing all below him. Frozeron upon his defeat was petrified and sealed away in the coldest of peaks.
![[Frozeron the Spiteful.png]]

## Thoraden the Cunning
Father of the Blue and attuned to the plane of Air (Storms) Thoraden is the most intelligent and manipulative of the four. Known to commonly decieve mortals with not even the most intricate of puzzles halting him for longer than a minute. Thoraden during the age of the Eladrin coated the entire WORLD in a thick and ever-lasting storms with hurricanes and tornadoes that roamed the plane and lightning that struck the same place however many times Thoraden willed Thoraden upon his defeated was petrified and sealed away at the highest of places guarded by the highest of kings
![[Thoraden the Cunning.png]]

## Acteroth the Vile
Father of the Black and attuned to the plane of Earth Acteroth is the smallest and considered the youngest of the Great Four. Yet Acteroth is the most insidious, with a breath of both poison and acid and the ability to burrow at high speeds. During the Age of the Eladrin he spread a great plague among the week, and caused the earth to tremble near non-stop. Acteroth upon his defeat was petrified and sealed away at the darkest depths of the world.
![[Acteroth the Vile.png]]

## Murath the Great Sleeper
Father of the Green and attuned to nature itself , Murath is said to have not participated in the Great Calamity of the Four Dragons, and is the "secret" fifth member. Murath slumbers within the Great World Tree and is allegedly harvesting power from the World Tree. Murath is worshipped by a cult of nature warriors and druids called "The Cult of the Green Sleeper" who have attempted many times to awaken Murath so that he may bring about the great reigning in of all of Vurendos.

# Crimson Transfusion
Necromancy
Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Target: Two Creatures within range that possess life.
Components: V S 
Duration: Instantaneous
Classes: N/A

You reach out to a creature's very life essence that binds them, and draw it from them to infuse another creature. You may choose a creature within range or yourself, the creature then must make a Constitution saving throw, or take 8d6 Necrotic damage. Whatever damage that creature took, is then transfused to a different creature within range, who then heals the damage taken. This doesn’t work on Constructs and Undead who lack blood.

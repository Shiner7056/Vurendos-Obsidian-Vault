Churral'ken stated that Orshal - God of the Crimson Legion was dethroned by a creature Akal'teroth - He is a Crimson Demon

Orshal was banished from the Crimson Realm - [[Crimson legion information]]

Churral'ken recognised the person within the ruins that has murdered every soul to enter. Her name is Afalri, she used to be a Ran'tal scientist. Her goal was to figure out how to stop the Crimson Legion. She was one of the few to research beyond the plane of existence, however she was most likely influenced by Alqi'vor. 

There seems to be no way to cure Afalri of her madness, Churral'ken seemed to not know.

Alqi'vor is able to release those inflicted by their void madness. It could be possible to snap Afarli out of her madness momentarily if we remind her of her heritage.

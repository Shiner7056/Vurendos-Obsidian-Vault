Raven Rot can spread from person to person under specific conditions, the dieses is based around conspiracy's so it can jump to another person if they believe the conspiracy is real and have been told about it from someone who has Raven Rot.

If their is no revolt or large conspiracy it can take from two weeks at the minimum from three to four months.

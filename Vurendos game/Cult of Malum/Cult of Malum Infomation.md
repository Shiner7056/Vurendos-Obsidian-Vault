#Malum

The Cult of Malum is one of the oldest Cults that have taken many forms

The Cult of Malum was around ever since the days of the Eladrin, during the time when the Four Dragons attempted to take over the Material Plane

They're a Cult who worships The Abyss and Malum, and are adept summoners

Their goal is to one day FREE Malum from his prison in Hades.

They wear dark robes and masks, and their idols are of a terrifying maw with no eyes

They are some of the least trustworthy people, yet have the power to back up any threats

#Extra [[Extra Information]] [[Astelrath Royal History]]

During the War of the Council soldiers commonly attacked their allies as they could not distinguish the difference between friend or foe. Mainly stemming from the inability to see what someone looked like belief a visor so a traditional visor salute was invented to discern yourself to allies.  

Lifting your visor was a sight of trust within your house as well as respecting those within it as well as a universal courtesy among warriors. Additionally it also proved their right hand was busy rather than holding onto their weapon they would be unprotected from any attacks.

The salute originated started within Baroness Halsia's house as she wanted a quick way for those to identify themselves to her.

Much later on into the war common foot soldiers started to salute as well, but without visors they instead lifted the metal of their weapon to their lips to align with the notion of being unprotected as a sign of peace. 

For a small time after the civil war when the slave rebellion started after Baroness Halsia was the reigning monarch, they adopted their own formal salute is opposition of the current monarch. The salute was the same as the sword salute but instead of raising their sword to make themselves defenceless they raised their tip of their tail to their lips as a sign as respect to other Tieflings. In modern practices Tieflings with swap between the tail salute for other Tieflings and use the formal salutes for others without tails as a sign of respect and unity.

While some of Astelraths citizens (Excluding Tieflings) have adopted their own way of saluting Tieflings it is not formally recognised as it is more a sign of respect within specific regiments where their commanders are Tieflings. They would instead place their right arm against to their heart with a flat palm.

#Extra 
In Astelrath there was a king. He was a good king. Until he was murdered. King Askilath the 1st was the 2nd longest reigning ruler. His son, ever the isolationist, went against his fathers policies of actually trying to make trade deals with the other nations. So he murdered his father and took the throne, becoming Askilath the 2nd. Askilath the 1st's daughter, Dalriath, signed a pact with a Fey to flee from Astelrath into the Feywild. 

  

1 Year into Askilath the Seconds reign, he was overthrown by Askilath the Firsts brother, Klioris. Who became Klioris the First, who after 2 months was killed by the priest of Vindicta for kin-slaying (by technicality). The priest crowned himself as King (King Zee'kal the Unholy) and then was assassinated a week later by another priest of Vindicta. However this priest did not claim the title, and instead crowned his daughter, Saalisri as the new Queen of Astelrath, which lasted for 6 months. 

  

The advisor at the time, who had seen 4 monarchs rule and die. Killed Saalisri in a "Hunting accident" and became King Valikalis who died to Saalisri's father, who then crowned the advisors brother as King. King Yorris then declared all Vindicta worship illegal, and killed about 70% of the priests in Astelrath for about 30 days, before he was murdered by an assassin hired by Worxanar. 

  

His brother, King Ucloris ruled for 1 day, before his sister killed him and became Queen Jala. Queen Jala then abdicated the throne to her brother Askilath the Third after realizing she was probably going to be the next target of assasination after only three days. Askilath after 2 months of his reign, made the unpopular policy of declaring it legal to worship Nihil, which caused his youngest son King Yorris the Second (who at the time was 14) to kill his father and take the throne.

  

Yorris the Second then legalized worship of Vindicta, even though the law ceased to be enforced anymore. He then died of food poisoning, and his 2 year old sister was thrown into the ocean for being 2 after only 23 days of ruling. 

  

A prince of Utoria, Prince Chalith, then attempted a minor invasion into Astelrath. He then declared himself as ruler, 2 days later the entirety of Astelrath entered a rebellion, and caused a civil war that lasted for 1 day. Chalith fled to Utoria where he was told he was a stupid idiot, and exiled to Jiothal where he was witch hunted and properly executed. 

  

Afterwards a descendant of King Valikalis returned and claimed he had the right to the throne. Then properly declared that the eldest son would receive the crown. King Valikalis the 2nd would then be killed by his uncle King Thetris 2 and a half weeks later, who was then killed by King Valikalis the 2nd's eldest son 5 days later, King Askilath the Fourth. He then died about a month later, which led his brother to become King Stevel, who died 2 days later. Luckily Stevel had an heir, who was 7, who became King Timothy, who then died because the Advisor thought he wouldn't be a good fit after 16 hours. 

  

Then back up the crown went to King Jahadis, who died 5 days later and had no heir. But he did have a lot of bastards. So Queen Tiyaka, a former indentured servant. Was lifted as Queen. She then ran off with half the Kingdom's wealth and settled in Dustalis after half a month. This caused all of the nobles to begin to squabble.

  

However, never to fear, the new King who had ties with Prince Chalith's family came along. Claiming HE was the Rightful ruler. He then gathered all of the nobles together, and said that they should all form a council that would vote on decisions. It then fell apart 2 days later. Lord Sequestis killed King Chalith the Second (because technically the Prince was king for like a day or two) and then claimed to be the new king. All the nobles agreed with this. Then they changed their minds after one day. 

  

All the nobles then disbanded the council, and began declaring war on each other. Plunging into a civil war that lasted for about 2 years. With Baroness Halsia coming out on top. Halsia opened up Astelrath's border just a bit, traded with the other kingdoms. And then was overthrown by a Tiefling slave uprising. The leader managed to storm the castle, and declare herself as Queen Sivina, who declared slavery illegal. Sivina was then killed by a Paladin of Vindicta, who was killed by her loyal slaves, who then all died to the guard. 

  

The Paladin then declared his daughter, Queen Xasia as Queen of Astelrath. The Paladin was later thrown into the ocean, and so was Xasia by Lord Overalkis, who became King. A rival noble lord then poisoned his food, and Overalkis survived. He then STABBED Overalkis but he survived and killed the noble lord. There were about 93 other assasination attempts made on Overalkis over the course of 63 days. 

  

Overalkis died in his sleep due to unknown circumstances (He did actually die of natural causes, but no one believes it). Lord Starian then ordained himself. He then started boasting about how he would be a great king. The General of Astelrath heard this, and only 2 minutes after his crowning, the General cut off his head and said HE was the king.

  

In the middle of the Almost-king Zaviat, he was killed by the person ordaining him for making his life the slightest bit more annoying. He then ordained himself, and then was killed 30 minutes later for killing the General by the General's advisor. Who spent 2 weeks trying to get himself crowned because he wanted the legitimacy, got crowned, and then executed a day later for "Being incompetent". 

  

Then King Jorisss the Snake King took over, ruled for 3 days. However a mysterious Sorceress killed him and revealed him as a worshipper of Fallaxa. The Sorceress then claimed she was from the original bloodline of Astelrath, and was a half-elf from the Feywild. She then ruled for a surprising amount of time. Making many good reforms, dying a year later because some racist in the higher ups who wasn't identified because he probably threw himself into a harbor after the fact, killed her. 

  
  

Then the son of Almost-king Zaviat took over. Ruling with an iron fist for 7 months. Then, King Gali'tath declared HE was the true king of Astelrath, and performed a great revolution to become the actual king. King Gali'tath was also a bit on the shorter side, and was made fun of by the royalty and nobility at the time.

  

King Gali'tath then ruled for 4 years, he was known as King Gali'tath the Long Lived, for having the longest rule, and King Gali'tath the Merciful. He was even better than the Sorceress Queen. With many positive reforms. King Gali'tath then died of "Unknown Causes". Many believe he was assassinated, however there's strong evidence to conclude he died peacefully from some other causes Astelrath couldn't identify.

  

Then his daughter, Queen Elithia ruled. However after 7 months she was caught in a scandal. Despite being an also good ruler she fell in love with a Slave. A Tiefling Slave. A Tiefling Slave where the love was mutual and actually consensual. So she and the slave were ambushed one day by HER OWN GUARDS and then put in a coffin and tossed into the harbor.

#Extra #Powerup 

I wanted to create this as a short 'guide' for Kalyth. I think I have been poor at communicating what I am going to find enjoyable and what I am looking for in the powerups instead of just saying, I want to be martial.

This is not intended to tell you what abilities to make or dictate exactly how her power ups should work, just to highlight what I will have the most fun with and to give you an easier time creating them.

I will also explain my thought process for choosing abilities because I realise that when I say. I want to focus on martial I should have explained it better.

---
**What did I intend for Kalyth to do in combat**

Kalyth was intended to be a versatile character, with several different different ways to contribute in combat:

*Martial*
-The main areas I looked to make this character as versatile as possible was to give her ways to be an effective martial. This was to make her being on the frontlines more viable. The martial was in my head suppose to be her being a Knight.
	-Having different ways to approach an enemy.
	-Protecting ally's that are behind me.
	-Having defensive and offensive martial options.
	-Having abilities that reward good decisions rather than just increasing numbers.

*Offensive Spellcasting*
-A side area that I wanted to play into was the spellcasting. While it is not the main aspect of the character I still want her to mix in her damaging spells. This is just so I am not doing the same thing each turn. As well as being able to change my playstyle, adapting to the situation. This gives me lots of enjoyment as it makes me think whether offensive spells are the best option in this moment.
	-Being offensive from range.
	-Changing focus from ally's back to myself.
	-Being able to disable enemies to give ally's more to focus on in combat. 

*Support*
-One of the main reasons I chose to play war cleric was so that I could have all of the healing options with the subclass entirely focusing on the martial side of the character. Kalyth can be the tank while not in health pool but the options the supporting spells gives me. Increasing my health, removing status effects or healing an ally to keep them in the fight. This is also backed up by some of my feats I choose. Being able to give myself a small amount of temp hp with my attacks or rather than using my bonus action to heal or attack with my war priest feature I can get another ally to attack.
	-Healing an injured ally.
	-Increasing both my survivability or any ally's.
	-Giving an ally another attack within the round.

*Overall*
Kalyth as you have said is not optimised to be able to martial and use spells. While true was never the point. In a round one is always meant to over shadow the other. Having to think about if melee is going to be the best option this round or if I should focus on healing, or if the target is to far away I need to swap to using my spells to increase my range. I think I should have been more clear with that.

Kalyth is meant to specialise in martial, however it was never meant to become her strongest option. Rather giving her more to do in combat, I said previously that I liked battle master, not because its good or super strong because it gives more options within combat.

In a turn I am only going to be able to focus on one of these at a time and that to me is what makes it so enjoyable. I cannot use a spell and attack in the same turn. There are some expectations with bonus action spells but for the most part these options are a one per turn thought. I cannot be expected to heal and be offensive two of the three need to be put down for a turn. That is where the versatility comes in. Rather than being super good at one of these, I am just okay at them. I am okay at melee attacking, I am okay at being an offensive spell caster. Healing Kalyth is great at. But its not something I always have to think about because other party members can heal themselves or reposition.

---
**What I enjoy in combat**
In combat I want to avoid that tunnel vision of focusing on one thing. I like making decisions, I like having to think about everything not just how I am going to deal the most damage. One of the reasons I enjoy cleric so much and have loved this subclass is that it makes me think during combat. I am not playing a fighter swinging 50 times and I am not a wizard using one super powerful spell. I have to think about if this turn its better to attack or support. Do my ally's need healing, are they afflicted by any statuses that need to be removed or is one of the enemies fleeing that needs to be killed or disabled.

---
**Themes for Kalyth inside combat**
Within combat I believe Kalyth to have three main themes, her martial aspects, her magical aspects and her support aspects. These themes to me should focus of a different aspect of her and should highlight different parts of what her character is. She is a Knight of Famis. I have been poor at explaining that. This is where I believe the major confusion comes from because these themes to me should work together as one rather than independently. Within combat  She has the options to either focus the round on magic, martial or supporting her ally's.

*Knight/ Martial*
Kalyth's martial to me focuses on what Aldwin has taught her, its something that fits with the martial the most and the main reason I went down the Squire/ Knight feats as they match the theme and the vision for Kalyth. They also give her those additional options within combat.

For the martial side being able to react to what the enemies are doing is part of the point. I am the frontline, so I want to be able 'hold the line' allows for allies to do most of the work. Kalyth's martial side is not meant to be a I can do super high damage but a defensive offensive mix. When she is engaged to a enemy I want to be able to focus on the whether the defensive option is the best or if I should be more aggressive.  

*Faith/ Magic*
Kalyths Magical side focuses on Famis, she cannot use magic without Famis. Throughout the role plays I have discussed how she knows very little about magic because she is exclusive to Famis, she knows what she can do and nothing else.

Her magic should focus on who Famis is. If you want magical abilities tie them to the cycle like for Avatar of Roses. Its a perfect fit to the character because it reflects that relationship between her and Famis. Because the she learned magic through Famis and martial through Aldwin her relation with Famis to me should be magical focused.



*Guardian/ Support*
Kalyths support side comes from both elements of being a Knight and a Cleric of Famis. She has feats that allow her to be upfront and martial while still supporting her ally's by giving them attacks, being a focus for the enemies. As well she has this role from Famis. Being able to heal, remove status effects or increasing AC for other characters. This is a theme that I think should include both her status as a Knight and a Cleric, allowing the best of both to shine through.

Her presence of the battlefield within the support role should reflect the two themes coming together to work in unison. The magical healing through Famis and the making an ally be able to engage more with the fight through the knight aspects. This is to me the main way of bridging the two together in combat rather than making her a Knight or a Cleric a Knight of Famis theming. 

*Overall*
While within combat I want to be able to do everything. I already can to how I will enjoy. I do not want any one theme to overshadow another. The martial side is a focus but for gameplay reasons not character reasons. 

Rather than having the powerups just affect Famis. I think its more interesting to include all of the themes for Kalyth. Not meshing all of these into a singular powerup but having each powerup focus on a different aspect. So one will focus of the Knight side of Kalyth, another will focus on her religious side and than another that focuses on the mix of the two.

Rather than having the power ups just affect her relationship with Famis, I think it would be incredibly rewarding to tie them to her different narrative milestones. For example: one power up focusing on her growth as a Knight, one focusing on her deepening faith, and perhaps a later one that bridges the two. This makes her mechanical growth feel directly tied to her roleplay journey.

I do not want every part of Kalyth's kit to work together perfectly. I like that they compete with one another.

**Checklist for power ups**
For the mechanical side of Kalyth to get as much enjoyment as possible I thought it may be important to make some form of checklist so I can clearly get what I will naturally choose to get the most enjoyment out of it.

I want to be able to make decisions in combat. I will naturally avoid a passive that increases numbers and go for something like a resource that lets me focus on one aspect of Kalyth for that round. 

Interesting over powerful if something sounds interesting I will choose it over something that allows me to win all my fights. I am playing Kalyth to support the team rather than to win all of her fights. Another thing that plays into this is avoiding tunnel vision on one specific aspect. If a power up allows most of my issues to be resolved with this ability It will never be chosen. Swapping between magical, martial and support was always the main idea but clearly I have done a poor job at explaining that.

I want to be able to react to what is happening in the fight, whether this turn I go and defending or use magic to heal or if I take a more offensive approach to attack or use the more offensive magic to deal damage to multiple targets.

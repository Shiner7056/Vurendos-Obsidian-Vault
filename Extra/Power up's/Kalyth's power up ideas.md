[[Power up Guideline]]

outline: For Kalyth I wish to play more into the martial side rather than the magical side and having it as a back. I do not want to entirely ignore using magic as it has been very useful but most of its uses have been out of combat so it will most likely stay that way.

I also do not want to buff Lass'mara anymore or have her as the main focus of strength rather than Kalyth as we have a lot of summons already and seeing as she gets player class levels and magical items its like I am playing two characters.

I also do not know how to balance these well so that's up to you to make changes.

## Idea one - Magical focus

Kalyth will gain the ability to infuse weapon attacks with her spells to gain the benefits of using a spell and attacking with a weapon at the same time.
**Spell-infused strike**
-When you take the attack action on your turn, you can cast a cleric spell of 1st level or higher, with a casting time of 1 action and infuse it into your weapon. If you do so, you make a single melee weapon attack as part of this action (forfeiting the benefit of your extra attack feature for this turn). On a hit, the target takes your normal weapon damage plus the effects of the spell. 
-If the spell requires a spell attack roll, you instead use your weapon attack roll. If the spell requires a saving throw, the target makes the save immediately upon being hit.
-If your weapon attack misses the spell slot is still consumed.
-Additionally you may also use your bonus action to use War Priest feature.

## Idea two - Support focus

Kalyth will be able to be more versatile with her War Priest feature being able to forfeit the attack to make the feature more interesting rather than just an additional attack.

Each one of these features upon use will consume a use of the War Priest feature as well as your reaction.

**Vanguard's Intercept**
-When a creature you can see attack an ally within your melee attack range you can use your reaction to make one melee weapon attack against the attacker. If your attack hits, the target takes the damage from the attack roll. The target will have to make a strength saving throw against your cleric spell save DC or be knocked prone or pushed 5 feet away, potentially interrupting the attack.

**Flash healing**
-When an ally within 30 feet of you takes damage from an attack or fails a saving throw on a turn other than yours, you can use your reaction to cast a cleric spell that restores hit points (For example healing word of cure wounds). The spell must have a normal casting time of 1 action or 1 bonus action, than it consumes a spell slot as normal.

**Shielding Priest**
-When an ally within 30 feet of you is targeted by an attack, you create a  divine barrier around the target ally. The target ally gains AC equal to your wisdom modifier against the attack. Potentially causing the attack to miss.

## Idea three - Support focus

**Guardians instinct**
-Whenever a friendly creature within 15 feet of you is targeted by a melee attack, you may use your reaction to move up to 15 feet towards them.
	-If you become adjacent to the creature that is attacking the ally you can cause the attack to target you instead. If you force the attack to target you instead gain +3 to your AC for the duration of that attack.
		-If the attack misses you, you may make a weapon attack against the target.

-You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. Regain all spent uses on a long rest.
